<objective>
Ship the identity spine of Phase 229: every authenticated session (guests included) is identified in Umami with the raw users.id taken from the JWT sub claim, at boot before the first pageview and again in ProtectedLayout with the account tag; impersonation never identifies; logout's hard navigation stays the reset. Alongside it, the disclosure and infra pieces that must ship with identity: the Privacy sentence, the pinned Umami 3.4.0 image, and the runbook steps for the image upgrade and for deleting an account's Umami rows.

Purpose: Umami knows pages but not people (0 of 1,941 app sessions carried a distinct_id in the 30 days to 2026-10-03). This plan makes sessions joinable to accounts without touching the backend (D-16) and without browser storage (D-08), and keeps the Privacy page truthful.
Output: lib/analytics.ts identity helpers, main.tsx boot call, App.tsx ProtectedLayout effect, useAuth.ts load-bearing comment plus a new useAuth test, Privacy.tsx sentence, docker-compose.yml pin, production-runbook.md Umami section.
