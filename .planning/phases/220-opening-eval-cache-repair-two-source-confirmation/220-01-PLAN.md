## Decisions recorded by this plan

- **OQ4 resolved — `opening_cache_audit.status` is `TEXT` + `CHECK`**, not
  `SMALLINT` + `IntEnum`, deviating from CLAUDE.md's high-cardinality rule at 2.57M rows.
  Reason: the seed and CACHEFIX-01 both lock `TEXT CHECK`; the eight values are
  operator-facing strings read directly out of psql during a multi-day run; the table is
  not query-hot (one indexed status lookup per stage); and the 2-byte saving is
  ~5 MB. The deviation and its reason go in the model docstring as an
  "Assumption Delta" paragraph (`app/models/herring_pool.py` pattern).
- **Pitfall 6 resolved — the `orphan` rule is refined away from the seed.** `orphan`
  means *no `game_positions` carrier row at ply 1..20 in any game*, not "not reached by
  the completed-engine-game walk". Measured on dev 2026-09-09: the seed's rule leaves
  39,207/106,615 rows carrier-less but only 24,029 truly have no carrier, so it would
  delete 15,178 rows (14.2%) that have a perfectly good board. Deletion is a separate
  explicit `orphans` subcommand with `--dry-run`, never an implicit tail of `screen`.
- **D-02 honoured** — no stage in this plan reads or writes `confirmed` / `n_sources` /
  `source_game_id`; those columns do not exist until plan 07.
