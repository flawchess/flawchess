<design_notes>
**Analyze uses `ply - 1`, not the seed's `ply`.** Verified in research (246-RESEARCH.md Q1, lines 114-137): `drill_items.ply = P` is the position after P half-moves, and `?ply=N` opens `mainLine[N]`, the position after move N has been played. So P-1 shows the card's board and P would show it after the mistake. Do not copy `FlawCard`'s `buildGameAnalysisUrl(flaw.game_id, flaw.ply)`: it passes the flaw ply on purpose.

**Choices adopted from RESEARCH under Claude's Discretion:** arriving-move highlight on (the solve screen shows the same move on this position, and it says nothing about the answer); board not clickable (Analyze is the single action, and a clickable board would need rework for Retry); no visible Retry slot (the action row is `flex gap-2` with Analyze `flex-1`, so a second button drops in later).

**One departure from PATTERNS.md.** It suggests separate mobile and desktop trees as in `FlawCard`. `FlawCard` needs them because its layout differs by breakpoint. D-05's card is horizontal at every width, and `useMiniBoardSize(DESKTOP_BOARD_SIZE)` already returns the right size at both, so this card uses one tree.

**`TrainArchiveResponse` is not added here.** It arrives with its only consumer in plan 246-05. Knip gates unused exported types, and plan 246-03's knip run falls between the two plans.
</design_notes>
