### Other findings

- **W-05 (test flake, new test):** `tests/routers/test_train_two_mode_streak.py::test_bc_compose_uses_settings_size` failed twice in four heavy `-n auto` batches with `409 {"detail":"No blunder-check sequences available"}`, and passed serially and in smaller batches. Cause: `compose_windows` draws random slot shapes from 8 seeded own games, so a draw can leave all three slots unfilled and returns an empty composition. Production is unaffected (foreign supply always exists), but the test can fail serial CI intermittently. Fix: seed the RNG or assert the spy count only and accept 409 when supply is thin, or seed more varied supply.
