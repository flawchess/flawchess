<objective>
Open blunder check to everyone (D-18), give users without rated games their stored level (D-17), and add the cross-mode
cooldown in both modes' selection (D-16).

Purpose: D-18 launches to registered users and guests in one release. Guests and users without rated games depend on
the foreign-blunder path at the right rating, and a flaw must not show up in both modes within a week.

Output: the beta gate is gone from the blunder-check router, the stored level replaces the 1500 default for users
without rated games, the cooldown filters sit in puzzle composition, the puzzle waiting count and the BC own pick, and a
measured latency for an 18-window foreign-only compose.
</objective>

## Assumptions surfaced for the owner

- A1: the level Elo values are exactly 1000 / 1500 / 2000 (D-17 says "about"); one constant to tune later.
- A7 (answers the CONTEXT discretion item): the cooldown runs from the LAST serve in local days via `session_date`, both
  directions, keyed on the flaw `(game_id, ply)`. It is not a whole-ladder exclusion: that would remove every active drill
  item from blunder check for good and shrink own supply for no stated goal. A blunder-check serve pushes a scheduled
  puzzle review back implicitly (the due item is filtered until the cooldown ends, then resurfaces most-overdue-first);
  `drill_items.due_date` is never written.
- RESEARCH Open Question 3 (inform only): a BC window built on a blunder the user has seen as a puzzle can be
  recognised. The 7-day cooldown limits the time overlap, not the recognition.
- A2 depends on the latency probe in Task 1: the launch slider maximum for blunder check is 18 windows (DB allows 30).
  If the probe shows an 18-window foreign-only compose above about 2 s or short sessions at the rating extremes, say so
  in the SUMMARY so plan 251-09 can lower the slider maximum constant.
