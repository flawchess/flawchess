<objective>
Add the set-8 hold-out per input (CONTEXT D-08) with its disagreement flag, and the owner section
the D-13 checkpoint reads.

Purpose: set 8 is the only fresh data, drawn where the candidates differ, so each input's whole
pipeline must be fitted without it and scored on it, and the owner must see when the set-8
result disagrees with the cross-validated choice of plan 05. The owner section lists what D-13
asks for that the labels and features can show: the commented label rows old and new, Window 14,
and the pure-miss pass rates. Runs on the committed tables before the labels exist; plan 07 runs
it for real.

Output: the 1-7 / 8 split constants (legacy split kept), `input_holdout`, the disagreement flag,
set-8 columns in the comparison table, `--labels` and the owner section.
</objective>
