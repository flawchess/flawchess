<objective>
Make the fit script fit any material input and either loss gate, fix review finding 254 WR-03
(CONTEXT D-12) and apply the WR-01 rule to the fit's rows (D-05), with candidates that carry their
own rows.

Purpose: the refit (plan 07) must compare the three inputs inside the full staged model (D-06,
D-14) on exactly the rows each candidate's shipped twin would score. This plan builds the
input-aware candidate and the per-candidate row filter; plan 05 adds the three-input comparison
and plan 06 the set-8 hold-out and the owner section.

Output: input- and gate-aware `CandidateSpec`, `_spec_exclusion`, stage-2 gate candidates, the
`--material-input` and `--loss-gate` flags, `PHASE_254_MODEL` and the 2026-11-15 reproduction
test. Until plan 05, `--material-input auto` fits the depth-3 input only (a functionality gap
plan 05 fills; no architectural change).
