<objective>
Client half of Part 2 of SEED-193: when the played move is one the server grades itself (a non-key
member of `server_graded_moves`), POST immediately with the payload tier and let the verdict render
from the SolveResponse after one round trip (D-09/D-10); keep the phone's after-move search running in
the background and send its 1.5 s reading on the review flush (D-12). Plan 04 already made the reveal's
game-move search queue behind the background grading (RESEARCH Pitfall 1); this plan proves that end to
end through the component and guarantees a background result never lands on the next puzzle (Pitfall 4).

Purpose: ~17% of SR + herring solves stop waiting ~1.5 s (plus a possible ~6 s re-check) for a grade
the server throws away, and those same solves produce the most valuable phone_grade rows (the
server's deep tier is free ground truth). Plan 06 renders the loading/failed line cards on top of this.

Output: `instantServerTier`, the instant branch in TrainSolveScreen, the late-record slot in the
telemetry hook, `ReviewRequest` typing, tests incl. mutation proofs.
</objective>
