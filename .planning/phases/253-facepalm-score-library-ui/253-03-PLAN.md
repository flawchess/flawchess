1. `FacepalmBadgePopover.tsx` (RESEARCH Pattern 4): module constants `HOVER_OPEN_DELAY_MS = 100` and `HOVER_CLOSE_GRACE_MS = 80` (the TagChip values) and two timer refs, both cleared on unmount. Trigger `onPointerEnter` and `onPointerLeave` act only when `event.pointerType === 'mouse'`, because touch fires pointerleave right after pointerup (it would close a just-tapped popover) and compat mouse events would arm a second opener (tooltip.tsx precedent, Pitfall 4); enter clears the close timer and arms the open timer, leave clears the open timer and arms the close timer. Content `onPointerEnter` (mouse) clears the close timer and content `onPointerLeave` (mouse) arms it. A `pointerDownRef` marks only a pointer press in progress: set it in the trigger `onPointerDown`; the trigger `onFocus` opens at once only when the ref is unset (keyboard focus); clear the ref in `onFocus`, in the trigger `onClick` and in the trigger `onBlur`. A tap (pointerdown, focus, click) then opens once through the click path, and a stale ref can never block a later keyboard focus: a pointerdown on an already-focused trigger is cleared by its click, a drag-off with no click is cleared by the blur that must precede any re-focus, and Safari (no focus on button click) clears it on click. This is a smaller route than also clearing on pointerup, which adds nothing since every re-focus is preceded by a blur. Tab-away: rely on Radix focus-outside dismissal; if the test shows it stays open, close on the trigger `onBlur` when `relatedTarget` is outside the content element.
