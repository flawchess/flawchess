<objective>
Re-reason guest lifecycle now that guests hold real Train state. Two rules, both written into
code AND into the tests that prove them (ROADMAP SC 8 asks for exactly that): the 30-day
inactivity purge takes the guest's `drill_sessions`, `drill_solves` and `train_settings` with the
games (D-08), and promoting a guest in place leaves every one of those rows attached (ROADMAP
SC 4). Plus the one stale invariant the gate removal left behind: the reminder fan-out's docstring.

Purpose: `/welcome` now promises "Nothing is deleted after 30 days of inactivity" as a sign-up
delta. That has to be a real difference, which means a purged guest's Train state must actually
go. And the score-screen sign-up ask promises "your streak and everything you have solved stay
exactly where they are", which has to be proven, not assumed.

Output: two deletes in the existing purge transaction, an inverted purge test with a filler-solve
case the games cascade cannot reach, and a promotion-preservation integration test.
</objective>
