**Phase Goal:** Make the facepalm score see missed material gains, with a weight (the loss-aversion knob) that each consumer sets. Today the score only measures what the played move gives away, so an obvious missed win of material scores near 0 (prod: 88% of allowed hanging pieces score >= 0.70 vs 21% of missed ones, at the same material swing).

## Goal Achievement

The machinery the goal asks for is in the codebase and wired: the score takes a missed-gain term weighted by a lambda that each consumer passes as its own named constant, computed at query time from stored facts, with the stored column dropped. The part of the goal that is not met is quantitative: the missed-material term earned almost no weight (0.0891), and the two example misses that motivated the phase still score about 0.15. The owner accepted this knowingly. See the end of this report for the honest reading.

### Gaps Summary

No FAILED must-have remains after accounting for the owner-accepted deviations. The status is `human_needed` because one runtime behavior (H1) is present and wired but unobserved on prod, and because the owner has not yet closed the question whether the shortfall below meets the intent of the goal.

**Honest reading of the goal.** What the phase delivers: the knob, the missed-gain term, the stored facts, query-time scoring, and a labelled refit. What it does not deliver: the goal's own headline example. Two observations support this and are not hidden by the accepted deviations:

- The two obvious missed wins that motivated the phase (817357:24, 820181:65) still score 0.15 and miss the 0.50 cut. The refit raised them roughly sevenfold, but not into the drill pool.
- Pure-miss pass shares on prod (calibration 2026-11-15) are 26.6% at 0.50 and 7.4% at 0.70, against 88.5% and 72.3% for losses. The cut moved from 0.70 to 0.50, which accounts for most of the improvement for misses. The fit report shows owner labels themselves call most pure misses unfair (21 fair vs 42 unfair among 63 `misses` rows), so part of the gap is the owner's judgement, not a model defect. But among the pure misses the owner called fair, only 12 of 21 pass at 0.50 (out of fold), and 2 of 21 at 0.70.
- The lambda knob moves scores, but only through a coefficient of 0.0891 times missed size capped at 5, so at most +0.45 in z. The signal that lifts pure misses is the best-move probability term, which lambda does not scale. A consumer that sets lambda to 0 still gets the best-prob term on every no-loss row, so "lambda = 0 reproduces a loss-only score" (D-05) holds only in the sense that the material term is dropped, not that the Phase 250 score returns. This is a consequence of the fit choosing `best_prob_for_misses` and is not recorded as a deviation in the SUMMARYs. It is worth stating to the owner when Noel Studer's loss-aversion answer comes in: lowering `FACEPALM_DRILL_LAMBDA` will have a small effect, because most of the miss-side weight is not behind lambda.
