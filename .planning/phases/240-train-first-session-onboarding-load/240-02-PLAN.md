<objective>
Make the first-ever Train session open on a sharp puzzle and put a "several" puzzle at puzzle 2 or 3, deterministically, without changing any later session (D-13, D-14, D-15).

Purpose: the first reveal carries tour part 1, whose result bubble teaches "only one" vs "several". A sharp first puzzle gives the real payoff and teaches the half that needs teaching; a soft or herring puzzle right after shows the other half while the tour is still running.

Output: one pure ordering helper, one EXISTS check, one type-classification query, one composition stage wired between assembly and materialization, and repository tests including a mutation-proven gate.
</objective>
