<objective>
Make `sacrifice` and `clearance` describe real tactics on 12-ply engine continuations rather
than on curated puzzle lines, and measure both so plan 06's clearance decision is a lookup.

Purpose: cook's `sacrifice` is "pov is down at least 2 points at any pov move from the second
onward", with no recovery test and no depth limit — 32% of cook's own sacrifice puzzles are
delayed recaptures, and in prod 78% of `sacrifice` tags (about 60,000 rows) fire in lines
where the tagging side is losing. cook's `clearance` is equally weak on real lines: 9 of 12
hand-reviewed dev tags were king retreats, pawn pushes or piece shuffles. D-05 adds a
persistence rule, D-06 a shared depth cap, and D-07 three clauses to clearance plus a
measured bar it has to clear to survive at all.

Output: two tightened predicates with the CONTEXT worked examples as fixtures, the
deliberate divergence documented where a future precision pass will read it, and the
post-strengthening clearance real-game real-share recorded as a number.
</objective>
