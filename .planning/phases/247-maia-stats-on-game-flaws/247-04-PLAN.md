<objective>
Wire the shared post-commit scorer into the two remote-worker write paths so prod games analyzed by remote workers
get Maia stats (D-08/D-13), and re-score after a tier-4 flaw-blob submit fills `missed_pv_lines` late so
`maia_second_*` gets filled on already-stamped games (D-14).

Purpose: most prod analysis arrives through `/atomic-submit` and tier-4 `/flaw-blob-submit`
(`app/routers/eval_remote.py`, not a services module). Without these hooks only the local drain would score, and
late blobs would leave the second pair NULL forever (RESEARCH Pitfall 2).
Output: two one-call hooks in the router (router stays thin) and endpoint tests.
</objective>
