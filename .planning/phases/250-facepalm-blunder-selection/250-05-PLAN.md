<objective>
Score every new game's user blunders in the existing post-apply Maia hook (D-07): reply spread from the post-blunder
inference it already runs, line facts from the stored line, and the facepalm score; store them on game_flaws and
stamp the game.

Purpose: new games arrive scored, so the selection has supply without waiting for the backfill; the backfill (plan
07) reuses exactly this scorer (D-08).
Output: hook changes in maia_flaw_scoring.py, FacepalmStats in maia_flaw_stats.py, stamp helpers and FACEPALM_COLUMNS
in game_flaws_repository.py, extended hook and lane tests. No frontend changes.
</objective>
