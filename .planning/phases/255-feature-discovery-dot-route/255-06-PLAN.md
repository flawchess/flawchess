personalised puzzle training on Train, Today, never when Train is opened. Draw the step's dot inside Train: on the
Today tab from the other Train tabs, and on the start button on Today.

the rule shape for the blunder-check step when it leaves beta (D-09).

the D-01..D-22 audit.
