## Planner resolution recorded here (refines the research recommendation)

223-RESEARCH.md recommends latching the threat probe once, at the player's commit, and
consuming it after the bot's reply. That latch is necessary (it is what proves the PLAYER
created the threat rather than it having been there all along) but on its own it can
announce a threat the bot has just neutralised, which is a board-truth violation in the
opposite direction. This plan therefore runs the probe at BOTH commits — both synchronous,
both on the exact position they mean — and fires only when the player created a threat AND
a bot piece is still hanging or under-defended after the bot's reply. Neither probe touches
the engine, so neither can leak engine knowledge. This is the reason `onMoveCommitted`
carries the live `Chess` instance.
