  ## Why this plan exists

  Plan 05 implemented D-05 exactly as CONTEXT locked it: a sacrifice's material
  deficit must still hold after the next pov move (`boards[k + 3]`), otherwise the
  line is a delayed recapture and the tag is dropped. Plan 05 measured the result
  honestly and flagged that `sacrifice`'s real-game real-share FELL (0.375 -> 0.222)
  instead of rising, and named row 0064 as the counter-example it could not resolve
  within its own constraints.

  Plan 06 then re-seeded the floor from that measurement (0.32 -> 0.17), which is the
  correct action IF the drops are correct.

  The D-13 operator spot-check (2026-09-13, `221-D13-SPOTCHECK.md`) established that
  they are not. Of the four contested rows:

  - **0064 and 0133 are genuine sacrifices that D-05 wrongly drops.** Both fail
    identically: material recovers on `boards[k + 3]` *because the sacrifice worked*
    (`Nxd6`, `gxf8=Q+` are the follow-ups the material was invested in). The rule
    cannot tell "returned because it worked" from "returned because it was a
    recapture" — the exact discrimination it exists to make.
  - **0134 was mislabelled `real`** and its drop is correct.
  - **0128 is unusable** (stored PV vs a fresh deeper search).

  So the 0.17 floor is a symptom being treated as the finding. Fix the rule.

  ## The lead worth checking first

  D-01 (this same phase, plan 04) added a per-tier winning floor at the firing node,
  in `forcing_line_gate.py`, with real eval access. D-05 was designed before D-01
  existed. A delayed recapture in a LOSING line is now already killed by D-01; what
  D-05 still removes on top of that is largely the WINNING delayed recapture — and
  that set is exactly where the two false drops live. Measure D-05's marginal
  contribution on top of D-01 before designing a replacement: the answer may be that
  the persistence test should be narrowed hard, or that it is now near-redundant.

  Note the layering constraint: `detect_sacrifice(boards, moves, pov)` has no eval
  access — eval lives in the gate. A discriminator that needs eval means plumbing, and
  plumbing eval into the detector is a bigger change than this plan should make
  unscoped. Prefer a board-derivable discriminator; if only an eval-aware rule works,
  say so in the SUMMARY and stop rather than widening scope.

  Candidate board-derivable discriminators, to be chosen on measurement, not taste:
  - The recovery overshoots the entry baseline (a real sac regains with interest or
    mates; a recapture returns to roughly even). 0064: 0 -> +1. 0133: 0 -> +10.
  - The opponent *chose* to accept (the pov move offered material and the opponent's
    reply was the capture) versus the pov capturing first and being recaptured.
  - The deficit's depth (0064 reaches -3, 0133 reaches -6) versus a recapture's
    shallow, symmetric swing.

  Whichever is chosen, both spot-checked rows must survive and the delayed-recapture
  shape must still be excluded, shown by measurement over the committed fixtures.

  ## What must not happen

  - Do not lower `REALGAME_REAL_SHARE_FLOOR['sacrifice']` to make the gate green. The
    floor follows the measurement; the measurement follows the rule.
  - Do not relax D-06 (the shared depth cap) or D-07 (clearance). They are not
    implicated by this finding.
  - Do not relabel any fixture row other than 0134, and do not adjust a label to move
    a real-share number. 0134 changes because the operator overturned it on the
    position, recorded in `221-D13-SPOTCHECK.md` before any re-measurement.
