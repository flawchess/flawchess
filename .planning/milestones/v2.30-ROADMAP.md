# v2.30 Blunder Check & Facepalm Score — Phase Detail

**Shipped:** 2026-11-19 (deployed to production, releases #464–#491)
**Phases:** 247–251, 253–256
**Full roadmap context:** [../ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)

Archived phase detail, moved out of the active roadmap at milestone close. The working
directories for these phases live in [v2.30-phases/](v2.30-phases/).

Nine phases that build blunder check, a new Train mode, and the facepalm score that picks its blunders. Phases 247 and 248 store Maia statistics and refutation odds on every mistake and blunder (SEED-261108-zkp, SEED-261108-dk0), Phase 249 ships the playable beta drill, Phase 250 replaces its findability rule with a facepalm score (SEED-261110-p7k), and Phase 251 turns it into a Train mode for everyone with a shared schedule, streak and leaderboards. Phase 253 shows the score in the Library (SEED-261114-0ke), Phases 254 and 256 refit it on missed gains and on material aggregated over depths (SEED-261114-mg7, SEED-261115-agg), and Phase 255 gives new accounts one red-dot route through the features (SEED-261115-d0t). Phase 252 (short engine line repair, SEED-261112-s4v) shipped its code in this range (releases #471–#474) but stays an active phase while the backfill runs.

### Phase 247: Maia Stats on game_flaws (SEED-261108-zkp)

**Goal**: Store Maia-3 statistics for every mistake and blunder in `game_flaws` (both players, 7.6M rows on prod),
scored at the mover's rating at game time. This is the data base for blunder check's face-palm selection
(SEED-261108-dk0, the next feature), a face-palm filter on the Flaws tab, and a future data story. Locked
decisions, measurements, traps and breadcrumbs in SEED-261108-zkp.

- **Columns (typed, nullable; not JSONB):** `maia_played_prob`, `maia_played_rank`, `maia_best_prob`,
  `maia_best_rank`, `maia_second_prob`, `maia_second_rank`, `maia_top_prob`, `maia_effective_moves` (2^entropy).
  "Best" and "second" are Stockfish's best and second-best moves at the flaw ply. No `maia_elo` column. Plus a
  per-game completion stamp mirroring `games.best_moves_completed_at` and its `is_maia_available` guardrail.
- **One inference per flaw:** a full-policy helper next to `score_move`; Elo via `pinned_elo_for_mover` (parity with
  the analysis-board Maia card), fed symmetrically as `elo_self` and `elo_oppo`.
- **Go-forward:** score flaws in the eval-apply path alongside the best-move pass (about 0.5-1 s CPU per game).
- **Backfill:** run locally on the dev machine against prod over the tunnel, 8-10 processes, ordered like the ES
  lottery's priority (recently active users, newer games first), deterministic, checkpointed and resumable. About one
  day (about 16 h compute + about 5 h of overlappable writes).
- **Traps to resolve in planning:**
  - Rebuild exact positions by replaying `games.pgn`, not from `game_flaws.fen` (piece placement only; Maia's
    softmax masks to legal moves, so guessed castling or en-passant rights skew probabilities).
  - Flaw reclassification (`_classify_and_fill_oracle`, a 4-way diff/upsert) must carry the Maia columns through,
    like the PV blobs, or re-analysis silently clears them.
- **Out of scope:** blunder-check selection, UI and threshold calibration (SEED-261108-dk0); the Flaws-tab filter
  UI; Maia WDL; full policy / top-k storage; Elo-ladder curves; Maia on non-flaw moves.

### Phase 248: Blunder-Check Refutation Stats and Calibration (SEED-261108-dk0)

**Goal**: Store how likely a player at the blunderer's rating is to find the punishment of each user-side blunder,
and calibrate blunder-check selection on prod data, so the selection phase builds on measured constants. Locked
decisions, prod measurements and traps in SEED-261108-dk0 ("Face-palm test v2", "Miss classification", "Precompute").

- **Columns (typed, nullable, on `game_flaws`):** `maia_refutation_prob` (REAL) and `maia_refutation_rank`
  (SMALLINT): Maia's probability and rank for Stockfish's best reply in the position after the blunder (opponent to
  move). Same rank definition as Phase 247.
- **Scope:** every user-side blunder (`severity = 2`, ply parity matches `user_color`), misses included (about 2.3M
  rows on prod). Not mistakes, not opponents' blunders.
- **Elo:** the blunderer's rating at game time via `pinned_elo_for_mover`, fed as both `elo_self` and `elo_oppo`.
- **Go-forward and backfill:** extend the Phase 247 scorer, lanes and `scripts/backfill_maia_flaws.py` pattern
  (local run against prod over the tunnel, sharded, checkpointed, resumable; about half a day).
- **Calibration report** (by Elo bucket, and by case: allowed / pure miss / mixed, see the seed):
  - share passing findability over a grid of R (refutation) and X′ (miss) thresholds;
  - mixed share among `is_miss` blunders (needs ES from per-ply evals with the correct ply convention);
  - effect of the eval band (pre-move ES bounds) on blunders and herring windows;
  - window fit under the hazard model (Good bar on the judged user moves before the blunder, positions 1-5) and
    supply per user after all filters, including the `MAX_MISS_SHARE` = 0.30 cap;
  - arrow-oddness diagnostic: Maia played-move probability of judged Good moves vs blunders (sample, Maia run in the
    report script);
  - recommended constants, plus an exported sample of about 20 windows per Elo bucket for a hand spot-check.
- **Traps to resolve in planning:**
  - Completion tracking: games already carry the Phase 247 stamp, so the new columns need their own completion
    signal (or a stamp version) to drive the backfill and go-forward catch-up.
  - Which `game_positions` row holds the post-blunder best move / PV (two eval ply conventions).
  - Rebuild positions from `games.pgn`; flaw reclassification (`_classify_and_fill_oracle`) must carry the new
    columns through.
- **Out of scope:** the selection service, session/scoring API, drill UI, schedule integration, leaderboards,
  cross-mode cooldown, any concreteness test (dropped), Flaws-tab filter.

**Depends on**: Phase 247 code (shipped). The calibration's miss numbers need 247-07's backfill at >=99% coverage.

### Phase 249: Blunder-Check Playable MVP (SEED-261108-dk0)

**Goal**: A playable blunder-check drill for beta users: select face-palm windows from the user's own games with the
Phase 248 constants, serve 6-window sessions, and run the drill (lead-in replay, arrow prompt "Safe / Blunder", 15 s
timer, points and forfeits, better-move chain, solution screen) on a beta-only Train tab, persisting every window and
answer so Phase 251 can build schedule, reviews and leaderboards on it. Locked decisions in 249-CONTEXT.md and
SEED-261108-dk0.

- **Selection:** hazard model (h = 0.265, L uniform 3-5), Good bar on every judged user move, eval band [0.10, 0.90]
  on pre-move ES, findability by case (allowed: refutation R = 0.3; pure miss: best move X' = 0.2; mixed: either).
  Missed punishments included with no session cap. Herrings from L Good in-band user moves. Own games first, then
  similarly rated users' windows when own supply runs out.
- **Drill:** 3-ply lead-in, ask before the move and before the reply, 1 point per correct "safe", 3 for a caught
  blunder plus 2 for finding the better move, false alarm ends the window, a missed blunder or a timeout forfeits the
  window's safe points.
- **Out of scope (Phase 251):** shared Train schedule and per-mode counts, streak, spaced-repetition reviews,
  cross-mode cooldown, Points and Score % leaderboards, guests and public launch.

**Depends on**: Phase 248 (refutation stats backfilled on prod, constants calibrated)

### Phase 250: Facepalm-Blunder Selection (SEED-261110-p7k)

**Goal**: Replace the Phase 249 findability rule (D-06) with a facepalm score per user blunder (0 = no chance to see
it at the mover's level, 1 = very careless), fitted on the owner labels from three stored facts: how rarely peers play
the blunder (`maia_played_prob`), how obvious the opponent's reply is (reply spread, full Maia policy at ply+1) and how much
material the stored refutation line wins within 3 plies (captures only, desperado and short-line handling, mate
counts). Selection keeps blunders at or above one score threshold. Also change the window mix: each window is, at one
third each, a red herring, a blunder from the user's own games, or a blunder from another user at the same level
(mover's rating within +-150); users without analysed games get foreign blunders only. No new Stockfish searches and
no worker lane. Locked decisions in 250-CONTEXT.md and SEED-261110-p7k.

- **Store:** reply spread and the line material result per user blunder on `game_flaws`, plus the derived
  `facepalm_score`; backfill prod; score new games in the existing post-apply Maia hook.
- **Select:** swap the blunder-check candidate predicate onto the score (one named threshold), implement the
  own / foreign / herring mix with herrings following the same own / foreign ratio, re-run the calibration and
  spot-check.
- **Clean up:** drop the nine Maia columns the score no longer reads (keep `maia_played_prob`) once the new
  selection is live and checked on prod.
- **Out of scope:** the library side-panel facepalm-score filter, opponent-side blunders and mistakes, refitting
  the score on drill answers (the threshold is set a little below the precision-optimal cut so those answers also
  cover blunders believed slightly too hard), rating-dependent line depth above 2000 (no labels there).

**Status**: Un-parked (owner, 2026-11-13): build now, including the prod backfill. Nothing waits for Phase 252;
once its prod pv repair is complete, a line-only pass recomputes the line losses, the mate ply and `facepalm_score`
(with the score refitted on the repaired lines). 250-CONTEXT D-18.
Phase 250 is complete (2026-11-14): plan 250-10 was not run and is superseded by Phase 254, which does the refit and
the line pass (254-CONTEXT D-11).

### Phase 253: Facepalm Score in the Library UI (SEED-261114-0ke)

**Goal**: Show the Phase 250 facepalm score to users in the library. A "Facepalm" range filter in the Tags side
panel for the Games and Flaws tabs, modelled on the tactic depth filter, and the score
as its own chip next to the Blunder badge on Flaws-tab cards (`<tree-palm> Facepalm 94%`, popover on the chip)
when the flaw has a score; after UAT also a mini-chip in the analysis move list and a line in the eval chart
tooltip (253-CONTEXT.md D-20 to D-23). Decisions in the seed and 253-CONTEXT.md.

- **Filter semantics** (owner, 2026-11-14): full range 0-100% = no filter; a narrowed range keeps only flaws
  with a non-NULL `facepalm_score` in range, so mistakes, inaccuracies and blunders without a stored line drop
  out. On the Games tab the range joins the single flaw `EXISTS`.
- **User blunders only.** Tag filters already apply to the user's flaws only (`player_only_gate`), so no
  opponent scoring and no backfill is needed.
- **Not a probability** (250 D-01): the chip reads as a degree; a short popover says it is the model's judgement
  and what a low value means. After UAT the card chip carries the word again (D-20).
- **Out of scope:** the score in the blunder-check solution screen; facepalm rate stats and opponent scoring
  (SEED-261114-iuv, after Phase 252).

### Phase 254: Facepalm Score Missed Gains and Loss-Aversion Knob (SEED-261114-mg7)

**Goal**: Make the facepalm score see missed material gains, with a weight (the loss-aversion knob) that each
consumer sets. Today the score only measures what the played move gives away, so an obvious missed win of
material scores near 0 (prod: 88% of allowed hanging pieces score >= 0.70 vs 21% of missed ones, at the same
material swing). Evidence, owner decisions and the proposed shape are in the seed; nothing below is locked
until the discuss phase.

- **Material:** `loss` as today plus `missed` (what the stored best line wins on top, depth 3, no engine);
  score input `loss + lambda * missed`. Lambda = 0 is today's score, lambda = 1 the symmetric swing.
- **Knob** (owner, 2026-11-14): wanted regardless. Whether blunder-check selection should be loss-averse is
  open; the owner asks GM Noel Studer. Only the default depends on the answer. The drill already asks "Good
  move or blunder?", so missed gains are valid items.
- **Pre-blunder Maia inputs** (owner: "we probably should"): Maia's probability of the best move and the
  pre-blunder policy spread, with a backfill for existing rows about the size of Phase 250's.
- **Labels and refit:** about 50 owner labels on misses, then a refit and a recheck of `FACEPALM_SCORE_MIN` and
  the drill pool size.
- **To settle in discuss:** fold Phase 250's open plan 250-10 (refit on repaired lines) into this phase's
  refit, so the score is refitted once; whether reply spread is dropped or down-weighted for pure misses.

**Depends on**: Phase 250 plans 01-09 and 11 (shipped), Phase 253 (shipped). The backfill and the refit wait
for Phase 252's prod line repair, because the new feature reads the stored best line; discuss and plan can
start before.
