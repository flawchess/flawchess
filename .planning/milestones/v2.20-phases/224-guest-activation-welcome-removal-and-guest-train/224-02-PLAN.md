<objective>
Lever A. Stop forcing a zero-game guest through an upsell-shaped interstitial before the product:
`Home.tsx` sends every zero-game account to `/library/import` (S-1), the localStorage dismissal
flag module is deleted, and `/welcome` is rewritten as the short four-delta "What changes when
you sign up" page that the phase's "Why?" buttons point at (S-5, D-05).

Purpose: prod shows 85% of registered users start an import versus 48% of guests (226/468), and
the entire gap sits at this first step. This plan is independently shippable and is the lever
measured by the guest import-start rate.

Output: one redirect rule for everyone, a deleted dismissal module, and a `/welcome` page that
is an explanation rather than a gate.
</objective>
