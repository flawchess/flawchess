<objective>
D-03 and D-13. Every Train string that presumes an analysis is running becomes honest for a
zero-game account, branching on two orthogonal flags — `hasGames` selects import wording for ANY
zero-game account, `isGuest` appends the sign-up ask, in that order (ROADMAP SC 7). And the Train
landing settings card, which D-01 has just exposed to guests, stops offering a reminder toggle
and a QR/install block the reminder fan-out provably never honors (D-13, GUESTACT-15).

Purpose: after Plan 01 a guest with zero games reaches a real Train session, and today that
session tells them "We're analyzing your games to find your blunders" when nothing is being
analyzed, and offers a reminder switch that will never fire. Both are silent lies shipped by the
gate removal.

Output: one audience type threaded through the pure copy layer, four audience-keyed copy records,
a guest-aware visibility resolver on the settings card, and three-audience test coverage.
</objective>
