<objective>
Author the phase's whole voice: nine more exhaustive per-persona line tables for the
in-game triggers, the roster greeting table, the daily roster rotation, and the roster
welcome bubble that replaces the prose intro card.

Purpose: this is roughly 240 authored strings across 24 personas. It gets its own plan
because copy authoring is a different kind of work from wiring, and because the
exhaustiveness guarantee is structural: `BOT_LINE_TABLES` is a
`Record<BotLineKey, Record<PersonaId, string>>`, so every widening of the key union must
land together with its fully-populated table or the build fails. Keeping that pair inside
one plan is what makes the guarantee hold continuously instead of only at phase end.

Output: the complete copy surface for the phase, its invariant test, the exported rotation
primitives that make "the same bot greets you on /train and /bots today" structurally true,
and the roster bubble.
</objective>
