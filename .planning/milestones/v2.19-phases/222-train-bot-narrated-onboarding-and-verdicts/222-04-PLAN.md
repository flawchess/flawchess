<objective>
Turn the tracer's single chat slot into the full solve-screen experience: the first-session
intro stepper, the drop-before-guess nudge, and the outcome-matched verdict with the action
buttons inside the bubble and the mute toggle retired.

Purpose: SEED-166's diagnosis is that a newcomer sees an unexplained quiz under a locked
board that snaps a dragged piece back silently. This plan closes both leaks on the solve
screen — the product finally states its premise (D-22) and no drop is ever rejected without
feedback (D-08) — and moves the reveal's actions into the bubble so the bot is the voice of
every state (D-10).

Output: a `TrainSolveScreen` whose entire narrative lives in one `TrainBotBubble` slot in
the measured board column, with `intro_seen_at` stamped server-side on completion.
</objective>
