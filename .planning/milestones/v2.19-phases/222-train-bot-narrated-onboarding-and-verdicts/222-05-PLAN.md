<objective>
Replace the score screen's "Session complete" heading with a bot bubble that states what
returns and when, explains why spaced repetition works, and only then asks for the reminder
— and give it the per-solve outcome list it needs to be truthful on both the live and the
resumed path.

Purpose: SEED-166's second leak is that the score screen closes a loop instead of opening
one — nothing says the missed positions come back, when, or why. D-25 is the copy that
fixes it. RESEARCH Finding C is the mechanical catch: `session.solved_results` is EMPTY for
a session composed and completed in one sitting, which is exactly the first-time-user
population this phase exists to serve, so a score bubble reading only `solved_results`
would tell every first-timer that nothing comes back. The live accumulator and the D-17
schema extension are both required, and this plan lands the accumulator.

Output: `solvedOutcomes` on `useTrainSession`, the data threaded through `Train.tsx`, and a
score bubble with four copy variants gated on `sr_explained_at`.
</objective>
