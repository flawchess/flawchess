<objective>
Record what was served: every composed blunder window carries its arm, the arm's sampling probability, a snapshot of
the facepalm score and its five inputs, and the type-line facts (deepest settled swing and mate on the facepalm-type
side), written at composition time. The pure feedback module also defines the read-time decision (type line literal,
hard to catch) that plan 06 puts on the wire.

Purpose: the score is a query-time expression that changes with every refit and a deleted foreign game NULLs
`game_id`, so the feedback and the later calibration must read a serve-time snapshot. This plan builds the record for
the existing (main-arm) draw; plan 03 adds the exploration arm on top and only has to set the arm.

Output: one migration, model columns and CHECKs, `WindowSelectionRecord` on `NewWindow`, the loader returning the five
inputs, `app/services/blunder_check_feedback.py`, tests.
</objective>
