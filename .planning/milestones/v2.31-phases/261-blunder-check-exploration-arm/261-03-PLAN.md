<objective>
Serve about 20% of blunder windows from a random draw with no facepalm-score cut: one coin flip per blunder slot, the
game-first probe without the cut on scored rows only, the cut moved from the loader and the judge to option time, batch
provenance so an exploration slot never draws from a cut-probe batch or through the cut, a scored probe that keeps
cut-loaded games in its frame, and the arm kept through every plan rewrite.

Purpose: answers can then validate or refit the score below the 0.70 cut (range restriction). The exploration arm must be
a plain sample of what the drill would serve with no cut, so its draw may not depend on the current score anywhere.

Output: rules, repository, candidates and selection changes with tests; plan 01's record now says EXPLORATION for these
windows.
</objective>
