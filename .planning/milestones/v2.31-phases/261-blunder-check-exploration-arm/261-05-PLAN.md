- `tests/scripts/test_caught_rate.py` (new)

Tolerance rationale (Claude's discretion, owner-overridable): exploration slots fill at least as easily as main slots
(no cut, bigger pool), so a served share below 0.18 points to an arm being dropped somewhere (Pitfall 1). Main slots at
the 0.70 cut can run short of supply and push the share above 0.20; above 0.26 would mean roughly a quarter of main slots
went unserved relative to exploration, worth a look. At n = 500 and p = 0.2 the 95% interval is about +/- 0.035, so the
rule fails only when the evidence clearly puts the share outside the band.
