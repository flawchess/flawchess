loss or missed moved by more than REVIEW_EPSILON, the reply probability coverage (known count and quartiles, information
for the owner), and up to REVIEW_EXAMPLES examples ordered by |delta missed| + |delta loss| descending then by n, always
including the rows of FIXTURE_LABEL_NS and the D13_GAME_PLY row when present; each example shows n, set, label, the
ANALYSIS_LINK, both lines, the per-depth old and quiet values with causes, and old vs new loss and missed. No usernames.
