<objective>
Show the facepalm rows of plan 05 as a "Facepalm blunders" card in You vs. your opponents: the
user's facepalm blunders per 100 of their moves next to their opponents' in the same games, in
total and per type, with the gap, both rates and the counts, a neutral bar and no verdict.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-11 (card placement, gate, TC switch), D-13 (neutral, no band),
D-14 / D-15 / D-16 (labels, hints, copy), D-04 (types never compared with each other), D-05
(no probability wording).

Output: `GapCell.noVerdict`, `FACEPALM_GAP_META`, two GapPopover props, the ContextTagCards
`trailing` slot, `FacepalmCard`, tests.

Split note (checker revision): this is the frontend half of the former plan 04; plan 05 delivered
the payload, the TS mirror and the fixtures this plan renders from. No backend file and no DB.

Recorded assumptions (Claude's discretion, confirmed at UAT):
- Copy (orchestrator decision 3, research A2): "Fewer is better." and the better / worse axis
  caption count as saying what a higher rate means (D-16) and are dropped for these rows.
- Neutral rows (orchestrator decision 5): a `noVerdict` flag on `GapCell`, so significant
  zoneless facepalm cells stay neutral while existing zoneless tactic cells keep their behaviour.
- Placement: after the second row of context-tag cards, one column from lg up, stacked last on
  phones; the heatmap slot and the card order stay as they are.
- A zoneless bar places a non-zero gap at the saturated edge (`gapPosition` with a zero band,
  today's tactic behaviour); the words line carries the size. Proportional placement needs a
  scale, which arrives with the benchmark zones (SEED-261120-bzq). Flagged for UAT.
- Significance wording (checker note, UAT item): "The gap is too large to be chance." and
  COPY_CHANCE state the significance of the gap, which D-13 allows; they say nothing about the
  score, so they are read as outside D-05's probability ban. The card-text regex checks
  /probab|likel/ and deliberately not 'chance', so this reading is confirmed by the owner in the
  Task 2 human-check, not by a test.
- Dev data: the opponent side is near zero on dev (144 of 5,624 opponent blunders scored for user
  28). The opponent-side visual read is a post-deploy prod check (plan 07).
</objective>
