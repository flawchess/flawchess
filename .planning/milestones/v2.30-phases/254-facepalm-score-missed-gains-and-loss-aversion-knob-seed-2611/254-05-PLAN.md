<objective>
The blind, stratified miss sampler for D-14 / D-15: a committed operator script that draws about 50 user blunders from
prod (mostly pure misses, some mixed rows), computes both line facts and the best-move probability locally, and writes a
blind labelling sheet like `leg2-labels-2026-11-11.tsv` plus a hidden key file. The orchestrator runs it on prod and the
owner labels in plan 07.

Purpose: the fit cannot learn missed gains from the 180 existing labels (17 no-loss rows only).
Output: `scripts/blunder_check/miss_labels.py` and its tests.
</objective>
