ELO Ratings and Results Breakdown: unchanged
Shared rules with the Endgame redesign (SEED-196):
  • One horizontal bar per metric, grey typical band in the middle, one dot coloured by band (red worse than typical, blue typical, green better). Better is always to the right, the same direction as the percentile bars, even though every metric here is a flaw rate where lower is better.
  • No red/green background halves, no CI whiskers, no zone-coloured numbers on the left. Statistics (events, 95% CI, p-value) move into the popover below a grey separator.
  • Outside the band the scale saturates (a gap 3× the band width lands near, not on, the edge), the same bounded feel as the 0-100 percentile bar. Big gaps never clip.
  • The difference reads as words ("1.7 fewer") instead of a signed number, which removes the "negative is good" decoding step.
  • Rows with fewer than 30 events on either side are faded (the endgame type table fades under 100 games).
