# Quick 261008-ob1: Train re-check also rescues off-key inaccuracy grades

## Why

Prod user 28, session 1360 position 1 (game 1694310 ply 21, key e5e4, played
b8c6): the phone's 1.5 s lite search read Nc6 at -0.8, a 0.087 ES drop against
the key, graded "inaccuracy" (1 move point). Full Stockfish depth 12-24 puts Nc6
at -1.19..-1.23 vs e4 -1.76..-1.79, a 0.047-0.050 drop, i.e. good (cutoff
INACCURACY_DROP = 0.05). `drill_solves.recheck` was null because
`shouldRecheck` only fires for a SHARP puzzle whose move graded GOOD (the
Phase 235 false-credit direction). Nothing rescues a false downgrade.

Owner decision (2026-10-08): reverse the Phase 235 "good only" scope for the
inaccuracy tier.

## Scope decisions

- Re-check an off-key, off-runner-up move graded `inaccuracy` on ANY keyed
  puzzle type (sharp, soft, herring): short-search noise is type-independent.
- `wrong` (mistake/blunder) stays out: a 1.5 s search rarely overstates a
  >= 0.10 drop enough to flip the verdict, and it would add the ~6 s wait to
  ~25% of solves.
- Sharp `good` trigger unchanged.
- D-11 unchanged: a completed re-check replaces the 1.5 s grade whatever it
  says (the deeper reading wins, in either direction).
- Outcome taxonomy unchanged: `confirmed` = the 3 s reading rates the played
  move good, `resolved` otherwise. The server's `_disagreement_accepted`
  requires the 1.5 s pair to be good too, so an inaccuracy upgrade never
  claims the sharp guess point; it only lifts move points via the client
  tier (path 3 of `_resolve_grade`). The two trigger populations stay
  separable in `drill_solves.recheck` by the 1.5 s pair (key_es - played_es).
- No schema/migration change; backend touches docstrings only.
