# Quick 261122-l3u: Keep the second-best move in the drain's opening flaw recovery

## Problem

In `_full_drain_tick` (`app/services/eval_drain.py`), a flaw ply in the opening region whose eval came from
the opening cache is re-searched by step 3b (`_fill_engine_game_flaw_pvs`, a MultiPV-2 search) to recover
its line. The search's second-best move was dropped. Step 3c (`_fill_engine_game_flaw_second_best`) was
meant to recover it, but selected its plies with `_missing_flaw_pv_targets`, which skips every ply that has
a pv, i.e. exactly the plies step 3b had just recovered. It never ran.

Result: node 0 of the flaw's line blob has a best eval and no second move, and the forcing-line gate reads
that as "only legal move". On prod, 5-8% of allowed and 8-11% of missed lines at ply <= 20 carry the gap
(last six weeks, about 3,500-5,000 lines a week). Only this path builds node 0 from `second_best_map`;
remote workers and the tier-4 re-walk search node 0 themselves.

## Tasks

1. `_fill_engine_game_flaw_pvs` takes `second_best_map` and records the second-best move of the search it
   already runs. Remove `_fill_engine_game_flaw_second_best` and its call (dead in the success path, and a
   second search otherwise). Renumber the step comments.
2. Tests (no DB, classifier and engine stubbed): second-best recorded for both recovered plies with one
   search per ply, nothing recorded on engine failure, the single-legal-move sentinel recorded.
   Mutation-check. `CHANGELOG.md` bullet.

Repairing existing rows: folded into Phase 252 (decision D-16, plan 252-10).
