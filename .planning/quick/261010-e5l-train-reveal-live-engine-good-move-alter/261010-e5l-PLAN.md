Purpose: a soft puzzle has several good moves, but the board shows at most one certified green alternative. The reveal engine already searches the root; widening that search to best + 3 and filtering its extra lines through the app's own severity classifier lets the board show the other good moves without ever certifying them (different hue, no badge, not in the "Also fine" text).
